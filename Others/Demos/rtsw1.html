    Build an economy, then cross a bridge.

    Drag to select workers. Right-click a crystal to mine. Use Q / W / F / E / X to place a Pylon, Barracks, Factory, Nexus or anti-air Turret (shoots helicopters from far outside their range). Train marines, rocket soldiers (area damage), tanks and helicopters and destroy the red Nexus on the far bank. On the spawn screen you can choose your faction and the enemy's: Terran (marines, rocket soldiers, tanks, copters) or Protoss (zealots, stalkers, immortals, colossi with regenerating shields).

    Smoke covers the map. Your units only see a short distance, so scout to find the enemy.
